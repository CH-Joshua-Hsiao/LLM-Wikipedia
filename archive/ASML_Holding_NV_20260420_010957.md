# ASML Holding N.V. (ASML)

## Overview

ASML Holding N.V. is a leading provider of photolithography systems essential for semiconductor manufacturing. The company plays a critical role in advancing technology through its development of cutting-edge lithography solutions.

## Financial Results for Q2 2025

ASML's financial performance in the second quarter of 2025 was robust, marked by substantial sales figures and profitability:

- **Total Net Sales**: EUR 7.7 billion
- **Net System Sales**: EUR 5.6 billion
  - **EUV Sales**: EUR 2.7 billion
  - **Non-EUV Sales**: EUR 2.9 billion
- **Gross Margin**: 53.7%
- **Operating Expenses (R&D + SG&A)**: EUR 1.5 billion
- **Net Income**: EUR 2.3 billion
- **Earnings Per Share**: EUR 5.90
- **Cash and Equivalents**: EUR 7.2 billion
- **Order Book (Backlog)**: EUR 33 billion

## Market Insights

Recent market trends suggest a significant impact of artificial intelligence on semiconductor growth. The demand in both Logic and Memory sectors is propelled by AI developments, which necessitate more advanced semiconductor nodes.

- **AI and Semiconductor Growth**: Artificial intelligence is a pivotal growth driver in the Logic and Memory sectors.
- **EUV Capacity and Expansion**: A projected 30% increase in EUV capacity is anticipated in 2025 compared to 2024.
- **China Market**: China's market share is expected to constitute over 25% of ASML's total revenue in 2025, highlighting its growing strategic importance.

## Business Outlook

ASML continues to invest in technological advancements and development, ensuring the company remains at the forefront of semiconductor innovation.

- **Technological Advancements**: Focus on advancing both low NA and high NA EUV products to optimize costs and enhance technology capabilities.
- **Customers and Demand**:
  - Strong sales within the Logic and Memory sectors.
  - Notable demand for EUV technology in DRAM and Logic nodes, especially fueled by AI-driven applications.
