## Overview

High Numerical Aperture Extreme Ultraviolet (High NA EUV) technology is a pivotal advancement in semiconductor manufacturing, designed to enhance accuracy and efficacy in chip production. This technology serves a critical purpose by reducing the necessity for multi-patterning layers, a common bottleneck in traditional semiconductor processes.

The shipment and installation of the cutting-edge EXE:5200B systems have commenced, marking a significant step towards widespread high-volume manufacturing implementation. These systems notably achieve a productivity benchmark of 175 wafers per hour, representing a 60% enhancement over previous models.

## Adoption and Customer Engagement

High NA EUV technology is in the R&D qualification phase, developed in close collaboration with industry stakeholders. There is a strategic roadmap in place, targeting the integration of High NA EUV technology into full-scale manufacturing, projected for 2026-27.

### Key Milestones
- Collaboration in R&D with industry partners.
- Progressive insertion of High NA technology into high-volume manufacturing processes.

## Impact on Semiconductor Industry

High NA EUV technology is set to revolutionize the semiconductor landscape by diminishing the reliance on complex multi-patterning processes, thereby lowering production costs considerably. As the industry shifts from low NA to high NA systems, this technology forms a cornerstone of that transition, enabling customers to reach more advanced technology nodes efficiently.

- **Cost Optimization**: Reduces production costs by minimizing intricate multi-patterning requirements.
- **Technology Evolution**: Facilitates the industry's strategic transition from low NA to high NA systems.

This leap forward supports broader advancements and innovations in semiconductor technology, firmly positioning High NA EUV technology as an essential tool in the next generation of chip manufacturing.
