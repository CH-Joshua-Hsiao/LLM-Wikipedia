## Overview

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSMC) is a world leader in semiconductor manufacturing, acclaimed for its pioneering advanced process technologies, including 3-nanometer and 5-nanometer nodes. TSMC is pivotal in driving innovation in the semiconductor industry, significantly impacting the markets related to Artificial Intelligence ([AI_Market_Impact](pages/AI_Market_Impact.md)) and High-Performance Computing (HPC).

## Global Expansion Strategy

- **US Expansion**: TSMC is undertaking a substantial expansion with advanced fabrication facilities in the United States. This move is driven by increasing [AI_Demand_Growth](pages/AI_Demand_Growth.md) and strategic governmental support to optimize [Supply_Chain_Management](pages/Supply_Chain_Management.md).
  
- **Japan and Europe Facilities**: Similar strategies are being executed in Japan and Europe, reflecting TSMC’s commitment to bolster its global production capacity. These efforts align with local markets' demand and the broader scope of [EUV_Technology_Adoption](pages/EUV_Technology_Adoption.md).

## Key Markets and Impact

TSMC's prominence in the AI and HPC sectors highlights its critical role in the ongoing [AI_Supercycle](pages/AI_Supercycle.md). The company consistently adapts to the dynamic technological landscape, supporting advancements and increasing efficiency in the semiconductor supply chain globally.

## Conclusion

With strategic expansion and cutting-edge technology adoption, TSMC not only secures its position as a leader in the semiconductor industry but also significantly contributes to shaping the future of technology across various sectors.
