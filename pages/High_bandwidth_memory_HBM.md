# High Bandwidth Memory (HBM)

**Type**: Concept  
**Sources**: [semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md](raw/semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview
High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) is a high-performance RAM interface designed for 3D-stacked SDRAM. It is essential for modern AI accelerators, such as those developed by [Nvidia](pages/Nvidia.md), to ensure processors are not starved for data.

## Market Dynamics
The expansive [AI Supercycle](pages/AI_Supercycle.md) has initiated an HBM "supercycle," propelling significant capital expenditure by major manufacturers like [Micron](pages/Micron.md), SK Hynix, and Samsung. As of 2026, the latest generation, HBM4, is at the forefront due to its enhancements in energy efficiency, which are vital for AI data centers constrained by power limitations. To produce HBM, manufacturers rely on advanced semiconductor equipment provided by companies such as [ASML](pages/ASML.md).
