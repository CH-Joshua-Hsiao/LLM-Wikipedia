## Overview

Applied Materials, Inc. (AMAT) is a major American corporation recognized as a leader in the materials engineering solutions industry. The company provides integral equipment, services, and software for the manufacture of semiconductor chips used in electronics, flat panel displays for computers, smartphones, and televisions, as well as solar products. Applied Materials plays a crucial role in global technological advancements through its comprehensive suite of products and services.
