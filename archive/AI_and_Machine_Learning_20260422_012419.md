## Overview
The utilization of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) forms a critical component of [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md)'s strategy. This approach is evident in the company's continuous innovation and integration across a variety of its product lines, such as the [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md) and Mac. AI is not only central to enhancing user experience but also pivotal in expanding the personal capabilities of Apple products.

## Strategic Importance
- **AI in Wearables:** The [Apple Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md) watch incorporates large-scale machine learning models specifically for health monitoring. A notable feature includes hypertension notifications, indicating the strategic importance of AI in preventive health care.

## Notable Innovations
- **Health Monitoring:** Leveraging AI in innovative health features like hypertension notifications.
- **Product Enhancement:** Integration of AI across Apple products, including [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md), Mac, and smart services such as Siri, enhancing their functionality and personal user experience capabilities.

The role of AI is therefore not just an add-on; it is a key driver in the evolution and functionality of Apple's product ecosystem.
