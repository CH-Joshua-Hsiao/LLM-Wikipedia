**Type**: Concept

**Source**: [Read More](.\raw\4835951_Apple_Inc_(AAPL)_Q4_2025_Earnings_Call_Transcript.json)

## Overview

The concept of supply chain shortage refers to circumstances where the demand for products exceeds the supply capabilities. This often results in potential revenue impacts due to unmet consumer needs and market demands.

## Key Details

- **Demand-Supply Discrepancy**: [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md) experienced significant supply constraints during the reported period.
  - The new [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md) models saw demand levels that outstripped the anticipated supply.
  - These constraints were primarily due to unpredicted levels of demand, highlighting a critical gap between consumer expectations and logistical capabilities.

- **Revenue Impact**: These shortages directly affected Apple's revenue potential, as the inability to meet demand led to missed sales opportunities.

By understanding the dynamics of the supply chain, companies can better prepare for future demand fluctuations and minimize the associated revenue risks.
