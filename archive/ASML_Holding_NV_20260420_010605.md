## Overview

ASML Holding N.V. (ASML) is a prominent player in the global semiconductor equipment industry. The company is renowned for its innovation in manufacturing photolithography equipment, particularly focusing on Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography and High Numerical Aperture (High NA) technologies. These technologies are critical for semiconductor manufacturing processes, enabling the production of smaller, more powerful, and energy-efficient chips.

ASML reported their Q2 2025 financial results on July 16, 2025. For more details, you can access the call transcript [here](https://www.asml.com/en).

## Key Details

### Financial Performance

As of the second quarter of 2025, ASML reported strong financial results:
- **Total Net Sales**: EUR 7.7 billion
- **Gross Margin**: 53.7%

These figures underscore ASML's leading position in the semiconductor equipment industry and its ability to capitalize on market demand.

## Technological Innovations

ASML's pioneering work in EUV and High NA technologies strengthens its competitive edge and drives the company's growth:

- **EUV Lithography**: A crucial technology in manufacturing advanced semiconductors, allowing for smaller, more powerful, and energy-efficient chips.
- **High NA Technologies**: Enhancements to EUV, offering even higher resolution capabilities critical for next-generation semiconductor devices.
