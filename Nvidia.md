# Nvidia

**Type**: Entity
**Sources**: [semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md](raw/semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview
Nvidia is the leading designer of AI hardware globally, closely tied to the massive [AI Supercycle](AI_Supercycle.md) currently sweeping the technology industry. 

## Financials & Products
* **Revenue**: Reached record annual revenue of $215.9 billion in fiscal 2026.
* **Hardware cycle**: Ramping up the next-generation Vera Rubin platform for 2H 2026, succeeding the Blackwell generation.
* **Projections**: Lifetime sales for Blackwell and Vera Rubin combined are projected to hit $1 trillion by the end of 2027.

## Supply Chain
Nvidia relies heavily on foundries like [TSMC](TSMC.md) and memory suppliers like [Micron](Micron.md) for High Bandwidth Memory ([HBM](High_Bandwidth_Memory.md)). Any bottlenecks at these suppliers threaten Nvidia's ability to fulfill its massive demand backlog.
