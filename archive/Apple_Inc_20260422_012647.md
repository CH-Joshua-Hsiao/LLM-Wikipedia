## Overview

[Apple Inc.](pages/Apple_Inc.md) has released its Q4 2025 earnings, showcasing strong financial performance. The company has not only significantly increased its revenue but also set records in its services sector, reflecting strategic growth and market dominance.

## Financial Summary

- **Total Revenue**: $102.5 billion, representing an 8% increase year over year.
- **Net Income**: $27.5 billion.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: $1.85.
- **Gross Margin**: 47.2%.
- **Operating Cash Flow**: $29.7 billion.
- **Dividends Paid**: $3.9 billion.
- **Services Revenue**: $28.8 billion, marking an all-time record.

## Key Personnel

- **[Timothy Cook](pages/Timothy_Cook.md)**: CEO & Director
- **Kevan Parekh**: Senior VP & CFO
- **Suhasini Chandramouli**: Director of Investor Relations

## Strategic Investments

Apple Inc. announced a substantial investment of $600 billion allocated towards:
- Advanced manufacturing.
- Silicon engineering.
- Artificial Intelligence (AI).

This investment underscores Apple's commitment to innovation and maintaining its competitive edge in the technology sector.
