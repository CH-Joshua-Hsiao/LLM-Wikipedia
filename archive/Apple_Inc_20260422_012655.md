## Overview

[Apple Inc.](pages/Apple_Inc.md) has released its Q4 2025 earnings, showcasing strong financial performance. The company has not only significantly increased its revenue but also set records in its services sector, reflecting strategic growth and market dominance. The iPhone, a flagship product of Apple Inc., continues to demonstrate its robust market presence and consumer demand. Its capability to overcome supply chain issues and still set revenue records highlights its market resilience.

## Financial Summary

- **Total Revenue**: $102.5 billion, representing an 8% increase year over year.
- **Net Income**: $27.5 billion.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: $1.85.
- **Gross Margin**: 47.2%.
- **Operating Cash Flow**: $29.7 billion.
- **Dividends Paid**: $3.9 billion.
- **Services Revenue**: $28.8 billion, marking an all-time record.
