# Micron

**Type**: Entity

## Overview
[Micron](pages/Micron.md) is a leading manufacturer of computer memory and data storage technology. It is currently a significant beneficiary of the [AI Supercycle](pages/AI_Supercycle.md) due to its role in producing [High Bandwidth Memory](pages/High_Bandwidth_Memory.md) (HBM).

## Manufacturing & Expansion
### High Bandwidth Memory
- **HBM4**: Micron's latest generation of HBM is gaining significant market traction, known for its superior energy efficiency compared to previous generations.

### Capital Expenditure
- **CapEx Plan**: Micron is executing a $25 billion capital expenditure plan in 2026. This includes building a new plant in Taiwan's Miaoli County, supporting scaling operations alongside giants like [TSMC](pages/TSMC.md).
