# TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company)

**Type**: Entity  
**Sources**: [tsmc-news-april-2026.md](raw/tsmc-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview

[TSMC](pages/TSMC.md) is a leading global semiconductor foundry, renowned for its advanced chip manufacturing capabilities. The company is currently experiencing significant demand due to the [AI Supercycle](pages/AI_Supercycle.md). This surge is largely driven by the high demand for advanced node chips, particularly in 3nm and 5nm technologies.

## Manufacturing & Expansion

TSMC's manufacturing operations are characterized by strategic global expansions and advanced technology initiatives:

- **Taiwan**: Serves as the core manufacturing base. The operations here are vital but are subject to geopolitical concerns that may affect the supply chain.
- **USA (Arizona)**: There is a significant expansion underway. Reports suggest a planned scale-up from 6 to 12 fabrication modules over the next 5 to 10 years.
- **Japan**: TSMC has confirmed plans to commence 3nm wafer production at a second Kumamoto facility by 2028.
- **Advanced Technology**: The company is set to enter volume production for [COUPE Silicon Photonics](pages/COUPE_Silicon_Photonics.md) starting in 2026.

## Financials & Risks

TSMC is projecting robust financial performance with an expected ~38% year-over-year revenue growth as of early 2026. However, several risks and challenges have been identified:

- Capacity bottlenecks pose a threat to AI chip production.
- Global supply chain disruptions may impact operations.
- Rising operational costs are anticipated due to the company's ongoing global expansion efforts.
