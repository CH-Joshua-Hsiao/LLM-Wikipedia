# ASML Holding N.V. (ASML)

**Type**: Entity  
**Source**: [Earnings Call Transcript](./raw/4801698_ASML_Holding_NV_(ASML)_Q2_2025_Earnings_Call_Transcript.json)

## Overview

ASML Holding N.V. stands as a leading provider of photolithography systems, integral to semiconductor manufacturing. Known for its advanced lithography machines, ASML significantly impacts the production of cutting-edge integrated circuits, crucially driving technological advancements and innovations in the semiconductor industry.

## Financial Results for Q2 2025

ASML's financial performance in Q2 2025 highlighted robust operational success, demonstrating strong sales figures and profitability:

- **Total Net Sales**: EUR 7.7 billion, underpinning substantial contributions from [High NA EUV Technology](pages/High_NA_EUV_Technology.md) lithography systems.
- **Net System Sales**: EUR 5.6 billion
  - **EUV Sales**: EUR 2.7 billion
  - **Non-EUV Sales**: EUR 2.9 billion
- **Gross Margin**: 53.7%
- **Operating Expenses**: 
  - **R&D**: EUR 1.2 billion
  - **SG&A**: EUR 299 million
- **Net Income**: EUR 2.3 billion
- **Earnings Per Share**: EUR 5.90
- **Cash and Equivalents**: EUR 7.2 billion
- **Order Book (Backlog)**: EUR 33 billion
- **Net System Bookings**: EUR 5.5 billion
  - **EUV Bookings**: EUR 2.3 billion
  - **Non-EUV Bookings**: EUR 3.2 billion

## Market Insights

The present market landscape emphasizes the eminent role of [artificial intelligence](pages/AI_Semiconductor_Supercycle.md) in the semiconductor industry's growth, especially within the Logic and Memory sectors requiring more advanced semiconductor nodes.

- **AI and Semiconductor Growth**: AI proves to be a pivotal growth catalyst in Logic and Memory domains, spotlighting the demand for more sophisticated technologies.
- **EUV Capacity and Expansion**: A forecasted 30% amplification in EUV capacity within 2025 compared to the previous year, predominantly spurred by AI-driven applications.
- **China Market**: China's market share, projected to exceed 25% of ASML's overall revenue in 2025, underscores its expanding importance.

## Business and Future Outlook

ASML is committed to sustaining its leadership stance in semiconductor innovation, with a clear emphasis on technological development and market expansion.

- **Technological Advancements**: Focused on refining low NA and high NA EUV products to reduce costs and advance technological capabilities.
- **Customers and Demand**: Observes strong sales within the Logic and Memory sectors, with notable demand for EUV technology in DRAM and Logic nodes.
- **Expected Full Year 2025 Revenue Growth**: 15%
- **Gross Margin Target**: 52%

The prospect for ASML remains promising, buoyed by continuous innovations in semiconductor technologies and the pervasive impact of [artificial intelligence](pages/AI_Semiconductor_Supercycle.md) across various industries.
