# ASML

**Type**: Entity
**Sources**: [semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md](raw/semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview
ASML is a Dutch company that holds a monopoly on Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography systems. These massive machines are absolutely essential for any foundry (like [TSMC](TSMC.md)) or memory manufacturer attempting to create advanced node semiconductors tailored for AI.

## Outlook & Risks
- **Demand**: Exceptionally strong demand from memory manufacturers producing [HBM](High_Bandwidth_Memory.md) (such as SK Hynix and Samsung).
- **Geopolitics**: Highly vulnerable to US export restrictions specifically targeting the sale or servicing of equipment to Chinese chipmakers.
