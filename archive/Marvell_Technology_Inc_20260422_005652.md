## Overview

[Marvell Technology, Inc](pages/Marvell_Technology_Inc.md). (MRVL) is a prominent player in the development of advanced semiconductor solutions, demonstrating remarkable expansion, particularly within its data center business. A key strength of Marvell lies in its optical components, significantly enhanced through the acquisition of Inphi. Under the insightful leadership of CEO and Chairman [Matthew Murphy](pages/Matthew_Murphy.md), Marvell continues to leverage cutting-edge technologies to enhance connectivity and data transmission capabilities, ensuring the company remains at the forefront of meeting the sophisticated demands of the industry landscapes it serves.

## Key Growth Areas

- **Optics Business**: The optics division is now a $3 billion franchise, exhibiting a fivefold growth since the acquisition of Inphi. It bolsters a strong position in the market with ongoing advancements in PAM technology, ensuring continued competitive advantage.

- **Custom Solutions**: Marvell possesses a robust pipeline for custom XPU (Expediting Processing Unit) and XPU attach products, with strategic growth anticipated beyond the year 2028. This aligns Marvell with future demands and reinforces its role in providing tailored, high-performance solutions.

- **Storage Solutions**: Marvell has significantly boosted its storage solutions sector, achieving an $800 million annual run rate. This success is attributed to its innovation in HDD (Hard Disk Drive) and SSD (Solid State Drive) controllers, pivotal technologies driving increased storage efficiencies.

## Financial and Strategic Outlook

Marvell forecasts continuous double-digit growth in all sectors by 2026, anchored by strategic partnerships and a pipeline of multi-generational design wins. These elements are central to sustaining Marvell's leadership in the competitive semiconductor market.
