## Impact on the Semiconductor Industry

The shift towards gate-all-around technology reflects the ongoing pursuit of improved electrical properties and scaling capabilities in semiconductors. By enveloping the channel with the gate, GAA transistors offer better control and reduced leakage compared to traditional FinFET designs. This makes them a key area of development as the industry pushes towards smaller nodes and higher performance chips.
