**Type**: Concept

## Overview

Onshoring initiatives have become a pivotal component in the modern landscape of technological advancement and economic strategy. This concept refers to the strategic move of bringing production and manufacturing processes back to the company's domestic country. The discussion around onshoring is embedded in larger dialogues about global economics, supply chain resilience, and national security.

## Key Details

- **Technological Advancement**: Onshoring is seen as a key driver for enhancing technological capabilities within a country. By localizing production, companies can foster innovation through closer proximity to research centers and better integration with local tech ecosystems.

- **Economic Strategy**: Economically, onshoring can help bolster a country's industry by creating jobs and stimulating local economies. It also reduces dependency on foreign production, especially in critical industries such as semiconductors and other high-tech fields.

- **Supply Chain Management**: Onshoring is often integrated into broader [Supply Chain Strategies](pages/Supply_Chain_Strategy.md), aimed at reducing risks associated with international logistics, tariffs, and geopolitical instability.

- **Impact on Semiconductor Industry**: The semiconductor sector is particularly influenced by onshoring trends. [Applied Materials Inc](pages/Applied_Materials_Inc.md), a significant player in the semiconductor manufacturing industry, has highlighted this as part of its operational and strategic focus during industry conferences held by [Goldman Sachs](pages/Goldman_Sachs.md).

Onshoring initiatives continue to reshape how industries think about production and strategic planning, serving as a cornerstone for modern economic policies and corporate strategies.
