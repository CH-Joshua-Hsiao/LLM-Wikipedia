# Gross Margin

**Type**: Concept

## Overview

Gross Margin is a key financial metric analyzed during company earnings discussions to assess profitability. In the context of Apple's recent earnings, it was a focal point during their [Q4 2025 Earnings Call](.\raw\4835951_Apple_Inc_(AAPL)_Q4_2025_Earnings_Call_Transcript.json).

## Key Details

- **Definition**: Gross Margin represents the difference between revenue and the cost of goods sold (COGS) divided by revenue. It indicates how efficiently a company uses its resources to produce and sell products or services.
- **Significance in Earnings**: During Apple's Q4 2025 Earnings Call, Gross Margin was a highlighted concept, reflecting its importance in evaluating Apple’s financial health and operational efficiency.
- **Application**: Investors and analysts often scrutinize Gross Margin to gauge how well a company manages its production costs relative to its net sales, aiding in investment decisions and company evaluations.
