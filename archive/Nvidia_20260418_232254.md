# Nvidia

**Type**: Entity  
**Sources**: [semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md](raw/semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview

[Nvidia](pages/Nvidia.md) is the leading designer of AI hardware globally, closely tied to the massive [AI Supercycle](AI_Supercycle.md) currently sweeping the technology industry.

## Financials and Products

- **Revenue**: Reached a record annual revenue of $215.9 billion in fiscal 2026.
- **Hardware Cycle**: Accelerating the next-generation Vera Rubin platform for the second half of 2026, succeeding the Blackwell generation.
- **Projections**: Combined lifetime sales for the Blackwell and Vera Rubin generations are projected to reach $1 trillion by the end of 2027.

## Supply Chain

Nvidia relies heavily on foundries like [TSMC](TSMC.md) and memory suppliers like [Micron](Micron.md) for [High Bandwidth Memory](High_Bandwidth_Memory.md). Any bottlenecks at these suppliers threaten Nvidia's ability to fulfill its massive demand backlog.
