# ASML Holding N.V. (ASML)

**Type**: Entity  
**Source**: [Earnings Call Transcript](./raw/4801698_ASML_Holding_NV_(ASML)_Q2_2025_Earnings_Call_Transcript.json)

## Overview

ASML Holding N.V. is a leading provider of photolithography systems essential for semiconductor manufacturing. Renowned for their advanced lithography machines, ASML plays a critical role in the production of cutting-edge integrated circuits, significantly influencing technology advancement and innovation in the semiconductor industry.

## Financial Results for Q2 2025

The financial performance of ASML in the second quarter of 2025 showcased solid results, underscoring substantial sales figures and profitability:

- **Total Net Sales**: EUR 7.7 billion, primarily driven by [EUV](pages/High_NA_EUV_Technology.md) lithography systems.
- **Net System Sales**: EUR 5.6 billion
  - **EUV Sales**: EUR 2.7 billion
  - **Non-EUV Sales**: EUR 2.9 billion
- **Gross Margin**: 53.7%
- **Operating Expenses**: 
  - **R&D**: EUR 1.2 billion
  - **SG&A**: EUR 299 million
- **Net Income**: EUR 2.3 billion
- **Earnings Per Share**: EUR 5.90
- **Cash and Equivalents**: EUR 7.2 billion
- **Order Book (Backlog)**: EUR 33 billion
- **Net System Bookings**: EUR 5.5 billion
  - **EUV Bookings**: EUR 2.3 billion
  - **Non-EUV Bookings**: EUR 3.2 billion

## Market Insights

Current market trends illustrate a pronounced influence of [artificial intelligence](pages/AI_Semiconductor_Supercycle.md) on the growth trajectory of semiconductors, particularly within Logic and Memory sectors, necessitating more advanced semiconductor nodes.

- **AI and Semiconductor Growth**: Artificial Intelligence acts as a pivotal growth driver in the Logic and Memory sectors.
- **EUV Capacity and Expansion**: An anticipated 30% increase in EUV capacity in 2025 compared to 2024.
- **China Market**: China's market share is projected to account for over 25% of ASML's total revenue in 2025, accentuating its burgeoning significance.

## Business Outlook

To maintain its position at the forefront of semiconductor innovation, ASML is steadfast in advancing technological developments:

- **Technological Advancements**: Emphasis on optimizing both low NA and high NA EUV products to reduce costs and elevate technological capabilities.
- **Customers and Demand**: 
  - Robust sales observed within the Logic and Memory sectors.
  - Significant demand for EUV technology in DRAM and Logic nodes, especially catalyzed by AI-driven applications.

The future outlook for ASML is optimistic, with continuous growth prospects propelled by innovations in semiconductor technology and the pervasive influence of artificial intelligence across diverse industries.
