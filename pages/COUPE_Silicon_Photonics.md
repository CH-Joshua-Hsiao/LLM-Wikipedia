# COUPE (Compact Universal Photonic Engine)

**Type**: Concept  
**Sources**: [tsmc-news-april-2026.md](raw/tsmc-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview
[COUPE_Silicon_Photonics](pages/COUPE_Silicon_Photonics.md) is a silicon photonics platform developed by [TSMC](pages/TSMC.md). It is designed to meet the growing industry demand for high-speed interconnects, which are essential for transferring massive amounts of data in AI and high-performance computing clusters. Volume production is slated to begin in 2026.

## Key Details
- **Purpose**: To support the increasing data transfer requirements in AI and high-performance computing.
- **Technology**: Silicon photonics, which leverages the use of light for data transfer, promising higher speeds and efficiency compared to traditional electronic systems.
- **Production**: Set to commence in 2026, indicating readiness for market deployment and potential industry impact.

COUPE's development is closely linked with the ongoing tech advancements in AI and semiconductor industries, potentially influencing companies like [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md), [Applied_Materials_Inc](pages/Applied_Materials_Inc.md), and [Nvidia](pages/Nvidia.md).
