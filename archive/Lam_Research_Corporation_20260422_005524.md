# Lam Research Corporation

**Type**: Entity

**Source**: [Lam Research Corporation - Earnings Call Transcript](.\raw\4832122_Lam_Research_Corporation_(LRCX)_Q1_2026_Earnings_Call_Transcript.json)

## Overview

Lam Research Corporation is a preeminent manufacturer of semiconductor processing equipment, instrumental in the innovation of tools crucial for semiconductor manufacturing processes. Focused on leading-edge deposition and etch technologies, Lam Research is aligning strategically with the burgeoning requirements of artificial intelligence and pursuing aggressive investments to preserve and enhance its market position. The company is also setting benchmarks in spares and services revenue growth.

## Financials

In the first quarter of 2026, Lam Research reported record revenues of $5.3 billion, demonstrating robust financial health supported by a gross margin of 50.6% and a 35% operating margin. Of significance is the revenue distribution across geographical markets: China led with 43% of total revenue, followed by Taiwan at 19%, and Korea contributing 15%. 

### Key Financial Metrics (Q1 2026)

- **Revenue**: $5.3 billion
- **Gross Margin**: 50.6%
- **Operating Margin**: 35%
- **Earnings per Share**: $1.15, ± $0.10

For the December 2025 quarter, Lam Research has set a financial guidance targeting a revenue of $5.2 billion, with an expected gross margin of 48.5% ± 1 percentage point, and an operating margin of 33% ± 1 percentage point.

### Business Segments

- **Foundry**: 60% of Systems Revenue
- **Memory**: 34% of Systems Revenue
  - **NAND**: 18% (see [NAND Market](pages/NAND_Market.md))
  - **DRAM**: 16%

## Technological and R&D Initiatives

Lam Research continues to drive forward with investments in innovative technologies such as **Vantex**, **Akara**, **Halo**, and **Dextro**. These technologies are critical in propelling the development and efficiency of semiconductor processes, emphasizing the company’s commitment to cutting-edge research and development.

## Shareholder Returns

Demonstrating a strong commitment to shareholder value, Lam Research executed $990 million in share buybacks and distributed $292 million in dividends in Q1 2026.
