## Overview

Apple Inc. reported remarkable financial results for the fourth quarter of 2025. The company achieved a record revenue of $102.5 billion, marking an 8% increase compared to the previous year and setting a new high for the September quarter. Notably, Apple's services division reached an all-time revenue record of $28.8 billion, reflecting a robust 15% year-over-year growth. The earnings call, which provided these insights, was conducted on October 30, 2025, with leadership led by CEO Timothy Cook and CFO Kevan Parekh.

## Key Financials

- **Total Revenue**: $102.5 billion USD
- **Services Revenue**: $28.8 billion USD
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: $1.85 USD
