## Overview

Applied Materials, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMAT) is a leading American corporation in the materials engineering solutions industry. It provides essential equipment, services, and software for manufacturing semiconductor chips used in electronics, flat panel displays for computers, smartphones, and televisions, as well as solar products. Recognized for its role in producing virtually every new chip and advanced display globally, Applied Materials is instrumental in technological advancements on a worldwide scale.
