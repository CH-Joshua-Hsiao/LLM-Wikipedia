## Overview

Kallisto is an advanced technology developed by [Lam Research Corporation](pages/Lam_Research_Corporation.md) with the goal of optimizing semiconductor manufacturing processes. This innovative concept aims to address and enhance several critical areas of manufacturing, ensuring the efficiency and scalability of production in the semiconductor industry.

## Key Innovations

- **Dielectric Etch Technology**: Kallisto introduces advancements in dielectric etch processes, which are essential for creating precise patterns and structures in semiconductor materials. These improvements contribute to the overall performance and reliability of semiconductor devices.

- **Metal Stacking**: The technology facilitates enhanced metal stacking techniques. This is vital for constructing multi-layered semiconductor components, enabling higher performance and more compact designs.

- **Advanced Node Scaling**: Bringing efficiency to advanced node scaling, Kallisto supports the transition to smaller, more efficient semiconductor nodes. This helps in maintaining Moore's Law by allowing more transistors to be fit into the same silicon die area, leading to faster and more energy-efficient devices.
