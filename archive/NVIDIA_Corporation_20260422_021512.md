## Overview
NVIDIA Corporation is a leading technology company specializing in the design and manufacture of high-end graphics processing units (GPUs) and system-on-a-chip units (SoCs). The company caters primarily to the gaming, automotive, and data center markets, providing cutting-edge solutions that enhance performance and efficiency.

## Q1 2026 Financial Highlights
NVIDIA has reported significant financial performance across its major segments for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026:

- **Gaming Revenue**: $1.2 billion
- **Data Center Revenue**: $500 million
- **Automotive Revenue**: $5 billion

These figures underline NVIDIA's robust market presence and continued growth, particularly in the automotive sector, which accounts for the largest portion of its revenue.
