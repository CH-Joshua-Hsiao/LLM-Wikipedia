# Apple Inc.

**Type**: Entity

**Source**: [Apple Inc. Q4 2025 Earnings Call Transcript](.\raw\4835951_Apple_Inc_(AAPL)_Q4_2025_Earnings_Call_Transcript.json)

## Overview

Apple Inc. reported strong financial performance in the fourth quarter of 2025, showcasing significant growth and setting records across various financial metrics and regions. The company's ability to consistently generate high revenue and earnings is reflected in its latest fiscal year-end results. A significant driver of this success is the [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md), which continues to exhibit substantial growth and technological advancement with the release of the iPhone 17 lineup in the September quarter of 2025.

## Key Financials

- **Quarterly Revenue**: Apple Inc. reported a Q4 2025 revenue of $102.5 billion, representing an 8% increase from the previous year. This marks a record for the September quarter.
- **Services Revenue**: The services segment achieved an all-time revenue record of $28.8 billion, with a year-over-year growth of 15%.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: EPS was recorded at $1.85, establishing a September quarter record.
- **Fiscal Year Revenue**: Total fiscal year revenue reached $416 billion, setting an all-time high for the company.
- **Regional Revenue Records**: Apple set all-time revenue records in key markets, including the United States, Canada, and various emerging markets.

## iPhone Performance

The [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md) continues to be a significant driver of revenue for [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md), demonstrating substantial growth and technological advancement. The release of the iPhone 17 lineup, which includes groundbreaking advancements such as the iPhone 17 Pro, underscores Apple Inc.'s commitment to innovation and market leadership in smartphone technology. This product launch has significantly contributed to Apple's robust market position and its capacity to innovate and expand its services and operations globally.

These financial achievements and product advancements underline Apple Inc.'s strong market presence and forward-thinking strategy in the technology industry.
