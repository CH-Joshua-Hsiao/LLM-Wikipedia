## Overview
The RTX 6090 Ti Ultra is a newly announced product by [NVIDIA Corporation](pages/NVIDIA_Corporation.md). It is designed as a high-end graphics processing unit (GPU) optimized for delivering exceptional performance in both computing and gaming environments.

## Technical Specifications
- **Transistor Count**: 5 Trillion  
  The RTX 6090 Ti Ultra is engineered with an immense 5 trillion transistors, enhancing its ability to handle complex calculations and multitasking with precision and speed.

- **Core Clock Speed**: 12.5 GHz  
  With a core clock speed of 12.5 GHz, this GPU offers remarkable speed, significantly reducing latency and boosting processing throughput for demanding applications.

- **Memory**: 256GB HBM4  
  It is equipped with 256GB of next-generation HBM4 (High Bandwidth Memory), providing vast memory bandwidth ideal for high-resolution textures and large datasets required in modern applications.
