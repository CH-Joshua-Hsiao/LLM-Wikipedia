# Apple Silicon

**Type**: Concept

## Overview

[Apple silicon](pages/Apple_Inc.md) refers to the series of system-on-a-chip (SoC) and system-in-package (SiP) processors designed by [Apple Inc.](pages/Apple_Inc.md) This innovative technology represents a significant shift from Apple's previous reliance on third-party processors to custom-designed silicon that powers a majority of Apple's computing devices. The implementation of Apple silicon is critical for enhancing the performance, efficiency, and capabilities of Apple's products, including Macs, iPads, and other devices.

## Key Details

- Apple silicon was a major topic of discussion during [Apple Inc.'s](pages/Apple_Inc.md) Q4 2025 Earnings Call.
- The transition to Apple silicon signifies Apple's intentions to achieve greater integration between hardware and software, tailoring performance and design to meet specific product needs.
- Benefits include improved processing power, energy efficiency, and advanced features such as machine learning capabilities that are optimized for Apple devices.
