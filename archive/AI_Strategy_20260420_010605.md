# AI Strategy

**Type**: Concept  
**Source**: [Apple Inc. (AAPL) Q3 2025 Earnings Call Transcript](.\raw\4807233_Apple_Inc_(AAPL)_Q3_2025_Earnings_Call_Transcript.json)

## Overview

[Apple Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md) perceives artificial intelligence (AI) as one of the most significant and transformative technologies of modern times. The company is actively integrating AI across its product lineup to enhance functionality and user experience.

## Initiatives

- **Apple Intelligence Features**: Apple has launched over 20 new intelligence features powered by AI, enhancing user capabilities across its product range, including the [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md), [iPad](pages/iPad.md), [Mac](pages/Mac.md), and [Apple TV](pages/Apple_TV.md).

- **AI-Driven Updates**: There have been substantial AI-driven updates across Apple's operating systems, including iOS and macOS, to drive improved performance, security, and user engagement.

- **Investment in Infrastructure and Talent**: Apple is making significant investments in AI infrastructure. This includes bolstering its talent pool to ensure the seamless integration of AI technologies and to maintain its competitive edge in the tech industry.
