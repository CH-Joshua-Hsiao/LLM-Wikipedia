## Overview

The iPhone continues to be a significant driver of revenue for [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md), demonstrating substantial growth and technological advancement with the release of its latest model, the iPhone 17 lineup, in the September quarter of 2025.

  - iPhone revenue hit an impressive $49 billion, reflecting a 6% year-over-year increase.
  - The September quarter witnessed a record-breaking revenue, propelled by the robust sales of the iPhone 17 models.

- **Product Highlights**:

    - Integrated with the advanced A19 Pro chip, providing unprecedented processing power.
    - Features an 8x telephoto camera that enhances photo-taking capabilities.
    - Acknowledged as the most powerful iPhone iteration to date, positioning itself at the forefront of smartphone technology.

The consistent growth in revenue and the groundbreaking advancements integrated into the iPhone 17 Pro underline [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md)'s commitment to innovation and market leadership in smartphone technology.
