# Micron

**Type**: Entity
**Sources**: [semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md](raw/semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview
Micron is a leading manufacturer of computer memory and computer data storage technology. They are currently a major beneficiary of the [AI Supercycle](AI_Supercycle.md) due to their role in producing High Bandwidth Memory ([HBM](High_Bandwidth_Memory.md)).

## Manufacturing & Expansion
- **HBM4**: Gaining significant market traction based on superior energy efficiency specs relative to previous generations.
- **CapEx**: Executing a $25 billion capital expenditure plan in 2026, which includes a new plant in Taiwan's Miaoli County to support scaling operations alongside giants like [TSMC](TSMC.md).
