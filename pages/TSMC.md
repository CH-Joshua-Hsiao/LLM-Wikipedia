# TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company)

**Type**: Entity
**Sources**: [tsmc-news-april-2026.md](raw/tsmc-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview
TSMC is a leading global semiconductor foundry. They are currently experiencing significant demand due to the [AI Supercycle](AI_Supercycle.md), largely driven by high demand for advanced node chips (3nm, 5nm). 

## Manufacturing & Expansion
- **Taiwan**: Core manufacturing base, though subject to geopolitical chain concerns.
- **USA (Arizona)**: Expanding footprint; reports suggest scaling from 6 to 12 fab modules over 5-10 years.
- **Japan**: Confirmed plans to begin 3nm wafer production at a second Kumamoto fab by 2028.
- **Advanced Tech**: Entering volume production for [COUPE Silicon Photonics](COUPE_Silicon_Photonics.md) in 2026.

## Financials & Risks
- Projecting ~38% YoY revenue growth (as of early 2026).
- Risks include capacity bottlenecks restricting AI chip production, global supply chain disruptions, and rising operational costs due to global expansion.
