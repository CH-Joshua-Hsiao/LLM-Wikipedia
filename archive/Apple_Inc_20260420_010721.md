## Overview
[Apple Inc.](pages/Apple_Inc.md) is a major leader in technology, consistently innovating and expanding its market presence both in the United States and globally. The company's latest financial results highlight its robust performance and strategic initiatives for future growth.

## Financial Performance
For the second quarter of 2025, Apple Inc. reported impressive financial metrics:

- **Revenue**: $95.4 billion, reflecting a 5% increase year-over-year.
- **Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: $1.65, showing an 8% growth from the prior year.

These figures underscore the company's continued strength in revenue generation and profitability enhancement.

## Strategic Investments
### U.S. Expansion
Apple Inc. has announced a significant investment plan worth $500 billion in the United States over the next four years. The aim of this initiative is to bolster Apple's infrastructural and operational capabilities across key states. The investment includes:

- Expansion of teams and facilities in:
  - Michigan
  - Texas
  - California
  - Arizona
  - Nevada
  - Iowa
  - Oregon
  - North Carolina
  - Washington

These efforts are intended to enhance Apple's manufacturing footprint and foster innovation hubs in strategic locations nationwide.
