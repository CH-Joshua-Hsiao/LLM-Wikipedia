# COUPE (Compact Universal Photonic Engine)

**Type**: Concept
**Sources**: [tsmc-news-april-2026.md](raw/tsmc-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview
COUPE is a silicon photonics platform developed by [TSMC](TSMC.md). It is designed to meet the growing industry demand for high-speed interconnects, which are essential for transferring massive amounts of data in AI and high-performance computing clusters. Volume production is slated to begin in 2026.
