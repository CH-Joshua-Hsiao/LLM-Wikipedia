# ASML

**Type**: Entity  
**Sources**: [semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md](raw/semiconductor-industry-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview

[ASML](pages/ASML.md) is a Dutch company that holds a monopoly on Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography systems. These massive machines are absolutely essential for any foundry like [TSMC](pages/TSMC.md) or memory manufacturer attempting to create advanced node semiconductors tailored for AI.

## Outlook and Risks

- **Demand**: There is exceptionally strong demand from memory manufacturers producing [High_Bandwidth_Memory](pages/High_Bandwidth_Memory.md), such as SK Hynix and Samsung.
- **Geopolitics**: ASML is highly vulnerable to US export restrictions, specifically targeting the sale or servicing of equipment to Chinese chipmakers.
