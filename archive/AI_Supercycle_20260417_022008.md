# AI Supercycle

**Type**: Concept  
**Sources**: [tsmc-news-april-2026.md](raw/tsmc-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview

The "AI Supercycle" refers to a prolonged period of extraordinary demand for high-performance computing hardware, driven by the rapid growth and deployment of artificial intelligence. This era marks a significant shift in technological advances and infrastructure needs, stimulating various sectors of the tech industry.

## Market Impact

This surge has severely constrained advanced foundry capacity. Leading manufacturers like [TSMC](pages/TSMC.md) are facing bottlenecks in fulfilling orders for advanced nodes such as 3nm and 5nm, which are required for AI chips. These constraints impact broader tech supply chains and push foundries toward aggressive and costly global expansion in an effort to meet the heightened demand.
