## Overview

[Marvell Technology, Inc.](pages/Marvell_Technology,_Inc.md) (MRVL) shared its optimistic growth outlook during a fireside chat hosted by [JPMorgan](pages/JPMorgan_Chase_&_Co.md). The company highlighted significant advancements across its data center services, custom AI silicon development, and optical networking capabilities, underscoring a robust growth trajectory and strategic expansion plans.

## Key Participants

- **[Matthew Murphy](pages/Matthew_Murphy.md)**: CEO & Chairman of Marvell Technology, Inc.
- **Ashish Saran**: Senior Vice President of Investor Relations
- **[Harlan Sur](pages/Harlan_Sur.md)**: Analyst, [JPMorgan Chase & Co](pages/JPMorgan_Chase_&_Co.md)., Research Division

## Discussion Highlights

- [Marvell Technology, Inc.](pages/Marvell_Technology,_Inc.md) has achieved a fourfold increase in its data center business over the past four years.
- The company anticipates significant growth within its optical franchise, projecting close to $3 billion in optical sales.
- Future strategies include advancements in electric-optics and an enhanced focus on scalable silicon architectures.
- Marvell is preparing for innovations in 2-nanometer and 1.6T optical production, positioning itself for long-term market leadership and scalability.
