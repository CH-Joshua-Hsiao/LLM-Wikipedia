## Overview

Apple Inc. reported strong financial performance in the fourth quarter of 2025, showcasing significant growth and setting records across various financial metrics and regions. The company's ability to consistently generate high revenue and earnings is reflected in its latest fiscal year-end results. Key products such as the [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md), [iPad](pages/iPad.md), [Mac](pages/Mac.md), [Apple Watch](pages/Apple_Watch.md), and [AirPods](pages/AirPods.md) are pivotal in driving this success.

### iPhone Performance

The [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md) continues to be a significant driver of revenue for [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md), demonstrating substantial growth and technological advancements. The release of the iPhone 17 lineup, which includes groundbreaking innovations such as the iPhone 17 Pro, underscores Apple Inc.'s commitment to innovation and market leadership in smartphone technology. This product launch has significantly contributed to Apple's robust market position and its capacity to innovate and expand its services and operations globally.

### iPad Impact

The [iPad](pages/iPad.md), a flagship product by [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md), continues to maintain its strong market presence with significant revenue contributions and technological advancements. This underscores its vital role in Apple's product lineup and overall financial health. The iPad’s continued innovation and strong sales performance illustrate its critical role within Apple Inc.'s ecosystem, making it a key product for both the company's financial success and its technological leadership.

### Mac Series

The [Mac](pages/Mac.md) series is an integral part of [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md)'s product lineup, marked by significant achievements in performance and market presence. The Mac's ability to integrate advanced processing technology positions it as a key attribute in Apple's portfolio, further enhancing the company's success and market footprint.

### Apple Watch

The [Apple Watch](pages/Apple_Watch.md) is a revolutionary wearable device designed to provide users with an integrated experience of health monitoring, communication, and connectivity. It is produced by [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md). The Apple Watch contributed significantly to Apple's financial performance, garnering a revenue of $9 billion. It remains a pivotal product in Apple's portfolio, consistently advancing with new features and technological enhancements to meet the growing demand for health-oriented wearable technology.

### AirPods

[AirPods](pages/AirPods.md) are wireless earbuds developed by [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md), known for their integration with the broader Apple ecosystem and innovation in sound technology. As a critical component of Apple’s audio products, AirPods contribute to the seamless connectivity and versatility that Apple offers across its devices, enhancing user experience through advanced sound features and wireless convenience.

## Key Financials

- **Quarterly Revenue**: Apple Inc. reported a Q4 2025 revenue of $102.5 billion, representing an 8% increase from the previous year. This marks a record for the September quarter.
- **Services Revenue**: The services segment achieved an all-time revenue record of $28.8 billion, with a year-over-year growth of 15%.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: EPS was recorded at $1.85, establishing a September quarter record.
- **Fiscal Year Revenue**: Total fiscal year revenue reached $416 billion, setting an all-time high for the company.
- **Regional Revenue Records**: Apple set all-time revenue records in key markets, including the United States, Canada, and various emerging markets.

These financial achievements and product advancements underline Apple Inc.'s strong market presence and forward-thinking strategy in the technology industry. The combined performance of the iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and AirPods, alongside service revenue, positions Apple Inc. not only as a leader in consumer electronics but also as a formidable entity in digital services and innovation.
