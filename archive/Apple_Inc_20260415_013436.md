## Overview

Apple Inc. presented their earnings report for the second quarter of 2025 on May 1st, illustrating key aspects such as revenues from their extensive product line and their commitment to innovation and sustainability.

## Financial Overview

- **Total Revenue for Q2 2025**: $95.4 billion
- **Services Revenue**: Achieved an all-time high of $26.6 billion, marking a 12% increase compared to the previous year.

## Products and Revenue

- **iPhone**: Generated $46.8 billion in revenue.
- **Mac**: Contributed $7.9 billion to total revenue.
- **iPad**: Accounted for $6.4 billion in revenue.
- **Wearables and Accessories**: Reached $7.5 billion in revenue.

## Key Products Launched

- **iPhone 16e**
- **M4-powered MacBook Air**
- **iPad Air with M3**

## Forward-looking Statements

Apple foresees continued revenue growth in the future, although they recognize potential challenges such as tariffs and broader economic uncertainties.

## Sustainability Initiatives

Apple continues to advance its sustainability efforts with an ambitious goal of achieving carbon neutrality by 2030.
