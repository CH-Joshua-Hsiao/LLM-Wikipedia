# DRAM Demand

**Type**: Concept

**Source**: [Deutsche Bank's 2025 Technology Conference Transcript](.\raw\4817731_Applied_Materials,_Inc_(AMAT)_Presents_at_Deutsche_Bank's_2025_Technology_Conference_Transcript.json)

## Overview
The demand for DRAM (Dynamic Random-Access Memory) is poised to escalate notably due to progressive developments in AI technology. This sector is projected as one of the most rapidly expanding markets in the upcoming five years.

## Key Details
- **AI Influence**: The growth of AI is a major driver for the increased DRAM demand. As AI applications become more sophisticated and widely adopted, the need for efficient and powerful memory solutions such as DRAM increases.
- **High Bandwidth Memory (HBM)**: This particular segment, closely tied to AI advancements, is expected to experience substantial growth with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 30%-40%.

The demand for DRAM illustrates a direct connection to the burgeoning [AI Demand Growth](pages/AI_Demand_Growth.md) and [AI Market Impact](pages/AI_Market_Impact.md), marking it as a crucial component in tech industry trends.
