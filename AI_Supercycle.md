# AI Supercycle

**Type**: Concept
**Sources**: [tsmc-news-april-2026.md](raw/tsmc-news-april-2026.md)

## Overview
The "AI Supercycle" refers to a prolonged period of extraordinary demand for high-performance computing hardware, driven by the rapid growth and deployment of artificial intelligence. 

## Market Impact
This surge has severely constrained advanced foundry capacity. Leading manufacturers like [TSMC](TSMC.md) are facing bottlenecks in fulfilling orders for advanced nodes (such as 3nm and 5nm) required for AI chips, which impacts broader tech supply chains and pushes foundries toward aggressive and costly global expansion.
