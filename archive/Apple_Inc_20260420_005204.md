## Overview

[Apple Inc.](pages/Apple_Inc.md) (NASDAQ: AAPL) continues to showcase robust growth and innovation. The company remains at the forefront of the technology sector with sustained performance across its business verticals, as highlighted in their recent Q2 and Q3 2025 earnings calls.

## Financials

- **Q2 2025 Revenue**: $95.4 billion, up 5% from the previous year. Growth attributed to both product sales and service enhancements.
- **Q3 2025 Revenue**: $94 billion, up 10% year-over-year.
- **Net Income (Q3 2025)**: $23.4 billion.
- **Diluted Earnings per Share (Q3 2025)**: $1.57, up 12% year-over-year.

## Key Products and Innovations

- **Product Revenues (Q2 2025)**:
  - **iPhone**: $46.8 billion, up 2%.
  - **Mac**: $7.9 billion, up 7%.
  - **iPad**: $6.4 billion, up 15%.
  - **Wearables, Home, and Accessories**: $7.5 billion, down 5%.
  - **Services**: All-time revenue record of $26.6 billion, up 12%.

- **Product Range**: The key product lineup, including the [iPhone](pages/iPhone.md), [MacBook](pages/MacBook.md), and [iPad](pages/iPad.md), remains a leader in consumer technology.

- **New Product Introductions**:
  - **iPhone 16e**: Continued innovation in the smartphone segment.
  - **M4-powered MacBook Air**: Enhanced processing capabilities with the new M4 chip.

## Strategic Initiatives

- **U.S. Expansion Plans**: $500 billion investment over four years in new jobs, state expansions, and a new Texas factory, strengthening U.S. presence and infrastructure.
- **AI and Machine Learning**: Significant investment in AI technologies, showcasing Apple’s dedication to integrating advanced machine learning capabilities across the product ecosystem.
- **Enterprise Expansion**: Increased focus on expanding in emerging markets and bolstering U.S. manufacturing.
  
## Key Announcements

- **WWDC**: Unveiling of new design features and AI capabilities across all platforms, reflecting Apple's commitment to pioneering technology advancements.
- **Tariffs**: Estimated $1.1 billion in tariff-related costs expected for the September quarter, highlighting challenges in [Tariff Related Uncertainty](pages/Tariff_Related_Uncertainty.md).

## Leadership

- **CEO**: [Tim Cook](pages/Tim_Cook.md)
- **CFO**: [Kevan Parekh](pages/Kevan_Parekh.md)

Apple's enduring focus on product quality, innovation, strategic investments, and market expansion solidifies its position as a dynamic leader in the technology sector.
