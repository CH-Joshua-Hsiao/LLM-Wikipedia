# Semiconductor Industry News (Nvidia, ASML, Micron, TSMC)

**Date**: April 6, 2026

## Nvidia (NVDA)
* **Continued AI Dominance:** Nvidia reported strong fiscal 2026 results, with record annual revenue of $215.9 billion. The company is wrapping up Blackwell production and ramping up production of its next-generation Vera Rubin platform, expected to ship in the second half of 2026.
* **AI Revenue Projections:** Projected cumulative lifetime sales for its Blackwell and Vera Rubin chips could reach $1 trillion by the end of 2027.

## ASML (ASML)
* **Strategic Demand:** ASML continues to see strong demand for its Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography systems, which are essential for manufacturing advanced AI chips. Major memory manufacturers, including SK Hynix and Samsung, have placed significant orders for these systems.
* **Geopolitical Challenges:** U.S. lawmakers have proposed new export restrictions that could further impact ASML’s ability to sell or service equipment to leading Chinese chipmakers.

## Micron (MU)
* **HBM Momentum:** Micron is benefiting significantly from the "supercycle" in High Bandwidth Memory (HBM), which is critical for AI infrastructure. The company’s HBM4 chips are gaining traction due to their energy efficiency.
* **Operational Updates:** Micron has announced a $25 billion capital expenditure plan for 2026 to expand its manufacturing capabilities, including a new plant in Taiwan's Miaoli County.

## TSMC (TSM)
* **Capacity Expansion:** TSMC received Taiwan government approval to deploy advanced 3-nanometer (3nm) processes at its second factory in Japan, with mass production targeted for 2028.
