# CapEx

**Type**: Concept

## Overview

CapEx, or Capital Expenditure, refers to the funds used by a business to acquire, upgrade, and maintain physical assets such as property, industrial buildings, or equipment. It is often used to undertake new projects or investments by a company.

## Key Details

- The term CapEx is prominently discussed in the Q4 2025 Earnings Call Transcript of [Apple Inc.](pages/Apple_Inc.md).
- Companies utilize CapEx to ensure the growth and expansion of their business operations. This can involve investment in new technologies and machinery or the upgrade and maintenance of existing infrastructure.

## Relevance

- In the context of [Apple Inc.](pages/Apple_Inc.md), CapEx likely plays a crucial role in managing their extensive supply chain needs, building infrastructure, and advancing technological capabilities, including the development of [Apple silicon](pages/Apple_silicon.md).
- Strategic CapEx decisions can impact a company's [Revenue Growth](pages/Revenue_Growth.md) and operational efficiency, leading to potential benefits in Gross Margin and [Customer Satisfaction](pages/Customer_Satisfaction.md).
