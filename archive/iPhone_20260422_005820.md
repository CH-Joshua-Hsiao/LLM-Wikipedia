# iPhone
**Type**: Entity

## Overview
Apple's iPhone continues its legacy as a flagship product with impressive financial and technical milestones. In the September quarter, iPhone achieved a record revenue of $49 billion, marking a 6% growth year-over-year. This success was achieved despite facing supply constraints.

## Key Details

### Product Information
The standout of the current lineup is the iPhone 17 Pro, described as the most powerful iPhone to date. It includes significant advancements in camera technology and processing capabilities:
- **Camera System:** Equipped with an 8x telephoto lens, enhancing the photography experience by providing high magnification and clarity.
- **Chipset:** Powered by the advanced A19 Pro chip, delivering superior performance and efficiency across all applications.

### Market Performance
The financial achievements of the iPhone underscore its market dominance and the ongoing high demand for innovation in consumer technology. Despite the hurdles in supply chains, Apple has maintained its growth trajectory through superior customer satisfaction and high upgrade rates.

## Conclusion
The iPhone continues to establish new standards in the smartphone industry with cutting-edge technology and consistent revenue growth. Its enhancements, particularly in the camera and processor departments, affirm the iPhone's role in setting benchmarks for competitors.
