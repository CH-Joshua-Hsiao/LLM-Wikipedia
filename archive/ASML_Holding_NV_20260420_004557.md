## Overview

ASML Holding N.V. (ASML) is a prominent player in the global semiconductor equipment industry. The company is renowned for its innovation and advanced technologies, particularly in Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography and High Numerical Aperture (High NA) technologies, which are critical for semiconductor manufacturing processes.

## Key Details

### Financial Performance

As of the second quarter of 2025, ASML reported strong financial results:
- **Total Net Sales**: EUR 7.7 billion
- **Gross Margin**: 53.7%

These figures highlight ASML's leading position in the semiconductor equipment industry and its ability to capitalize on market demand.

### Leadership

ASML's executive team includes key figures leading the company towards sustained growth:
- **Christophe D. Fouquet**: President, CEO, and Chair of the Board of Management
- **Jim Kavanagh**: Vice President of Investor Relations
- **Roger J. M. Dassen**: Executive VP, CFO & Member of the Management Board

## Technological Innovations

ASML's pioneering work in EUV and High NA technologies strengthens its competitive edge and drives the company's growth:

- **EUV Lithography**: A crucial technology in manufacturing advanced semiconductors, allowing for smaller, more powerful, and energy-efficient chips.
- **High NA Technologies**: Enhancements to EUV, offering even higher resolution capabilities critical for next-generation semiconductor devices.
