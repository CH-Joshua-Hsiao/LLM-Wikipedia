## Overview

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSM), known globally as TSMC, is one of the leading semiconductor companies in the world, renowned for its advanced chip manufacturing capabilities. It plays a pivotal role in the technology supply chain, serving major clients such as [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md), [Nvidia](pages/Nvidia.md), and [TSMC](pages/TSMC.md).
