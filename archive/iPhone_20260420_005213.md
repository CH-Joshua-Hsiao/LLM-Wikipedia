## Overview

The iPhone continues to be a significant product line for [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md), consistently contributing to the company's overall revenue growth. The release of the latest iPhone iteration, the iPhone 16 family, has brought substantial advancements and sustained the device's popularity globally.

## Financials

- **Revenue Q2 2025**: iPhone revenue soared to $46.8 billion, demonstrating robust demand for the new models. This financial performance underscores the product's hefty share in Apple's revenue mix.
- **Revenue Q3 2025**: iPhone revenue reached $44.6 billion, marking a 13% year-over-year increase, further reinforcing the device's strong financial impact.

## Technology

- **New Features**: The iPhone 16 family introduces several technological enhancements, including the innovative A18 chip, which delivers increased processing power, and the C1 modem, noted for enhanced battery efficiency. These upgrades are aimed at providing a superior user experience.

## Market Performance

- The iPhone continues to see **double-digit upgrader growth** throughout the quarter, highlighting its strong position in the market and the loyalty of its user base. The iPhone 16 family demonstrated strong double-digit growth, contributing significantly to Apple's record-setting upgrader rates for the quarter. This growth trend is indicative of the strategic success in both product innovation and brand loyalty cultivation by [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md).

The groundbreaking improvements in the iPhone 16 have cemented its status as a leading smartphone in the competitive technology landscape, further solidifying [Apple_Inc](pages/Apple_Inc.md)'s influence and market impact.
